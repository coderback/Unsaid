Which one is right?

Fifteen disclosure units. For each, you see what a bank said in year 1 and the closest things it said in year 2. Decide what happened to it.

This is deliberately blind. You are not told what the LLM judge said, what Claude said, or which units they disagreed on — seeing that would anchor you and waste the exercise.
  • RETAINED — the same risk, still disclosed, wording may differ
  • REWORDED — same substance, materially rewritten
  • SOFTENED — still there, but hedged, shortened or made less specific
  • REMOVED — the risk is gone, nothing in year 2 covers it
  • ABSORBED — folded into a broader or different disclosure
  • UNSURE — genuinely cannot tell. Use it; it is data, not failure.
