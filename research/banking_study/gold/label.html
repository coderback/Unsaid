Your task. Read the Year-1 disclosure, then the Year-2 candidates retrieved for it. Decide what happened to that specific disclosure in Year-2. You are seeing exactly what the model saw. Its answer is hidden on purpose.

  • RETAINED — same risk, same substance and specificity.
  • REWORDED — same risk and same specifics, different wording only.
  • SOFTENED — risk downgraded or hedged, or specific quantitative disclosure (a figure, table, sensitivity metric) dropped while the topic survives.
  • REMOVED — the specific disclosure is absent from Year-2.
  • ABSORBED — the same substance, including its specifics, genuinely appears inside a broader Year-2 disclosure. Same topic alone is NOT absorption.
  • UNSURE — genuinely ambiguous, or the evidence is too mangled to judge. Use it freely; these are excluded rather than counted as errors.
The key question is what a reader loses going from Year-1 to Year-2 — not whether the subject is still mentioned somewhere. Candidates are retrieved by similarity, so a related Year-2 unit almost always exists even when the real disclosure is gone.
